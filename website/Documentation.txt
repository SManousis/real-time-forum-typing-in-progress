WebSocket models

Message types expected:

- "new_post"  - always open
- "new_dm_count" - always open, by client
- "status_update" - subscription su:id //for comments, posts and dms: reactions and in the case of posts commentcount
- "user_update" - subscription os:id
- "new_comment" - subscription nc:id
- "new_dm" - subscription dm:id
- "typing" - subscription dm:id


Notifications:

- New Post (Id, Title, Body Preview, ReactionCount, Categories, Creator, CommentCount, CurrentUserReactions, CreatedAt, Truncated)
- New Unread Dm Count (Id, ConversationId, Creator)
- (Reactions on owned content)
- (Comments on owned posts)


Subscriptions 

HOME: 
(older subs may close if limit exceeded)
- POSTFEED: Reaction/comment count on loaded posts su:id (of post)
(older subs may close if limit exceeded)
- USERS_FEED: Loaded user online status os:id (of user)

DM:
(closes on section change)
 - CONVERSATION : New dms and typing status in open conversation dm:id(other user when sending from website) dm:smaller_id:larger_id(in server)
(closes on section change or if limit exceeded)
 - DM: Reaction on loaded dms su:id(of entity)


POST:
(closes on section change)
- SINGLE POST: New comments on open post nc:id (of post)
(closes on section change or if limit exceeded)
- COMMENTS: Reaction on loaded comments su:id (of comment)
